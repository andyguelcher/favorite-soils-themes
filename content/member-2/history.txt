Optional. The stages of how your soil formed, played by the "Play its history" button on your 3D model. Leave this file without any stages and the button does not appear.

Each stage starts with a line beginning "Stage:". Use the same horizon names as your soil.txt.
- When: when it happened, in words.
- Age: about how many years ago, as a number (used to order the timeline).
- Deposit: horizons laid down at this stage.
- Develop: horizons that start to form.
- Finish: horizons that reach how they look today ("all" means every horizon).
- Text: what happened, in a sentence or two.
- Source: where the facts come from. Check every date and fact against its source, and cite it in Sources on your page.

An example stage, shown here with # in front so the site ignores it. Remove the # signs to use it:
# Stage: The glacier lays down till
# When: about 17,500 years ago
# Age: 17500
# Deposit: 2C, 2Cd
# Text: The ice sheet spreads clay loam till across the area.
# Source: Official Series Description
