Timeline editor

Build the stages that "Play its history" shows on a soil's 3D model. Nothing here changes your site until you paste the result into that soil's history.txt.

1. Choose a soil

2. Edit the stages

Stages play in order, top to bottom. For each one, write what happened, then click the horizons in its soil column to show what the model should look like at that point. Each click moves a horizon to the next step:

  • Not there yet: hidden in the model.
  • Laid down: appears as raw, pale parent material.
  • Starts forming: partly turned into the horizon it is today.
  • Finished: looks as it does today.

A horizon can only move forward in time: making it later in one stage updates the stages around it to match. The last stage always shows the soil as it is today.
